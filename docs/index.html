What it does

Works everywhere One click darkens any site, even ones that never built their own dark mode.
Photos stay normal Smart color handling keeps images and video looking right — no "negative" effect.
Tune it to your eyes Brightness, contrast, warmth (sepia), and grayscale sliders, live-previewed.
Per-site overrides Dark everywhere except your bank's site? Set it once, it's remembered.
Auto day/night schedule Optional — dark mode switches on at night and off in the morning, automatically.
Nothing collected No analytics, no tracking, no network requests. Settings stay in your browser.
