## Name

## Short description (≤132 chars, Chrome limit)
Force dark mode on any website. Tune brightness/contrast, keep photos in color, and schedule auto dark mode by time of day.

## Category
Chrome/Edge: Accessibility or Tools. Firefox: Appearance / Photos & Media (pick "Appearance").

## Full description

```
Blackout turns any website dark — instantly, on every page, in
every tab.

No more squinting at bright white pages at night. No more hunting for a
"dark mode" toggle buried in some sites' settings while others never bother
to add one at all. Blackout works everywhere, the same way,
automatically.

WHAT IT DOES
• One click enables dark mode on the current site or your whole browser
• Smart color handling keeps photos, videos and images looking normal —
  no "photo negative" look
• Fine-tune brightness, contrast, sepia warmth, and grayscale to match your
  eyes and your monitor
• Per-site overrides: love it on Wikipedia but want it off on your bank's
  site? Set it once, it's remembered
• Optional day/night schedule — dark mode turns on automatically at night
  and off in the morning
• Settings sync across your signed-in devices, no account needed
• Export/import your settings as a backup

PRIVACY FIRST
Blackout collects nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no
network requests. It only touches the page to apply a style filter, using
storage your browser already provides. Full privacy policy: see the
PRIVACY.md in the extension's homepage/repo link.

WORKS EVERYWHERE
Available for Chrome, Edge, and Firefox (Safari coming soon), sharing the
same look and settings design across all of them.

Free to use. If you find it useful, a rating helps a lot — thank you!
```
